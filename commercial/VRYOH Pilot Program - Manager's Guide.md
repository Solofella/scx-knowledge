What is VRYOH?

Approval workflow

Three Approval Options
Your options for managing draft responses:
01
APPROVE
When: The tone matches your brand, no factual errors, acknowledges the guest's concern appropriately, no placeholders need filling
Action: Click the Approval Status cell → Select "Approve" → The draft is flagged for publishing
02
EDIT & APPROVE
When: The structure is good but tone needs adjustment, a detail is wrong, you want to add a personal touch or offer, one sentence doesn't match your voice
Action: Click the Final Response cell → Paste the VRYOH draft and make edits → Click Approval Status → Select "Edit & Approve"
Example: Original: "Please reach out if you'd like to plan another visit." → Your edit: "We'd like to make it right — dinner on us your next visit. Ask for [Your Name] at the host stand."
03
REJECT (Not Accepted)
When: The response doesn't sound like you, it ignores the core complaint, it's defensive or dismissive, it makes a promise you didn't authorize
Action: Click Approval Status → Select "Not Accepted" → Leave a brief note
What happens: You'll get a follow-up from the VRYOH team with a revised draft within 24 hours

Draft Tiers Explained
TIER 1 - STANDARD
Review Type: Positive or simple
What You Do: Approve quickly (2 min)
Examples: Straightforward positive reviews, simple requests
TIER 2 - CALIBRATED
Review Type: Mixed signal (happy + frustrated)
What You Do: Spend 5 min; edit if tone is off
Examples: "Great food but slow service", "Nice place but expensive"
TIER 3 - DIGNITY-RESTORATION
Review Type: Negative or crisis
What You Do: Read carefully; approve or customize
Examples: Safety concerns, discrimination, serious complaints, dignity-risk situations

How to use it:
If you have T3 drafts, read those first
T2 drafts usually need the most customization
T1 drafts are safe to approve quickly

Expression Mode — What It Means
1
EXPLICIT
What It Is: Said directly
Example: "The service was terrible"
Use it: High Explicit = Guests are clear. Easy to respond to.
2
IMPLICIT
What It Is: Obvious but not stated
Example: "We waited forever"
Use it: High Implicit = VRYOH is finding things guests didn't say directly. This is where intelligence adds value.
3
MASKED
What It Is: Said positive but meant negative
Example: "Nice place for what it is"
4
PERFORMATIVE
What It Is: Obligatory praise; something bothered them
Example: "Everything was fine" (flat)
Use it: High Performative = Guests aren't being honest. Something is off; investigate.
5
CONFLICTED
What It Is: Genuinely mixed feelings
Example: "Loved ambience, food was disappointing"
6
ABSENT
What It Is: No emotion; just facts
Example: "We came Tuesday. It was quiet."

Quick FAQ
Q: How long per draft?
A: 2–3 minutes. Don't overthink it.
Q: Can I batch approve?
A: Yes. 15 min for 5–10 drafts.
Q: Tone is wrong?
A: Edit it. Your voice matters.
Q: Where do I publish?
A: We can do it (you give login once) or you copy/paste to each platform.
Q: Missed 48-hour window?
A: No penalty. Respond when you can.
Q: Can drafts publish without you?
A: No. You approve everything.
Q: What if I disagree with the signal?
A: Trust your judgment. Reject + note your reason.
Q: Volume spiked to 20 reviews?
A: Approve T3 first, then T2, then T1. Aim for 48hr but don't panic.
Q: What if only 1 review comes in per day?
A: All email sections still appear. Each review has signal, tier, domain, and expression data. Even 1 review triggers full intelligence.

Post-Pilot: Platform Access Requirements
If you continue VRYOH service after the 60-day pilot, automated daily delivery requires API access to your review platforms. This ensures seamless, real-time data collection for accurate insights and timely response drafting. Understanding these requirements is key to a smooth transition from pilot to full service.
Google Business Profile
API Access: Required
Setup: Grant VRYOH read-only access to your Google Business Profile via Google Business API.
What you'll do:
Authorize VRYOH as a trusted third-party app in your Google Business settings.
Grant "read reviews" permission only (no editing or publishing permissions).
Timeline: Approximately 10 minutes, a one-time setup.
Security: Access is fully revocable anytime directly from your Google account settings.
Yelp
API Access: Required (if available)
Setup: Provide read-only access to fetch reviews automatically.
What you'll do:
Provide your Yelp Business ID.
Authorize VRYOH via Yelp's third-party integration process.
Timeline: Approximately 10 minutes, a one-time setup.
TripAdvisor
API Access: Required (if available)
Setup: Read-only access to fetch reviews automatically.
What you'll do:
Authorize VRYOH via TripAdvisor's API partner program.
Grant "read reviews" permission only.
Timeline: Approximately 10 minutes, a one-time setup.
OpenTable
API Access: Requires VRYOH technology partnership (in progress).
How OpenTable works: OpenTable requires VRYOH to become an approved technology partner before any restaurant can grant API access. VRYOH is actively pursuing this partnership, with an estimated 3-6 month approval timeline.
During Pilot (Day 1-60): VRYOH collects OpenTable reviews manually via web monitoring. No action is required from you; we handle all data collection, and you'll still receive drafts in your approval sheet.
Post-Pilot: If the OpenTable partnership isn't yet approved when you continue service, VRYOH will continue manual collection at no additional cost until API access becomes available.

Overall Timeline for API Connections
During Pilot (Day 1-60): VRYOH handles ALL data collection manually across all platforms. No API setup required from your end. No action needed from you.
Post-Pilot (Day 61+): If you continue, we will schedule a 20-minute call to authorize API connections for Google, Yelp, and TripAdvisor. OpenTable will continue via manual collection until partnership approval is finalized.
Security & Privacy Assurance
Your data security is paramount. All API connections established by VRYOH are designed with robust protections:
Read-only: VRYOH cannot edit, delete, or publish content on your behalf.
Revocable: You can disconnect VRYOH's access anytime from your platform settings.
Encrypted: All data transmission occurs via secure, industry-standard protocols.
Monitored: VRYOH logs all API access, maintaining comprehensive audit trails for transparency.
