## Spend Control Rules

### Rule 1: No Category Overruns Without Justification

If any category exceeds budget by >20% in a given month, document reason and adjust following month.

**Example:** Marketing in Jun budgeted $100, actual $150 → reduce Jul to $0 to compensate.

---

### Rule 2: Contingency is Last Resort

$25/mo contingency is NOT discretionary spend. Use only for genuine emergencies or unplanned costs.

**NOT allowed:** "I want a new tool, use contingency."  
**Allowed:** "DigitalOcean increased prices mid-month, use contingency to cover gap."

---

### Rule 3: Revenue Does Not Justify Cost Increases

If Pilot 1 converts early and generates revenue in Jul, **do not** increase marketing spend in response.

Maintain budget discipline. Any surplus goes to cash reserves, not immediate reinvestment.

---

### Rule 4: Defer Nice-to-Haves

**LangSmith Plus** activates May only if stabilization gate passes on schedule. If stabilization delays to Jun, defer LangSmith to Jun.

**Paid outreach** budget (Mar–Jun) is discretionary. If warm network yields Pilot 1 without spend, redirect to savings.
