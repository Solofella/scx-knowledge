## 4. Pilot Offer Design

### Pilot 1 Structure (First Client Only)

**Duration:** 30 days

**Fee:** $500 flat (or in-kind for testimonial + data rights — decision pending)

**Deliverables:**
- 4× Weekly Operational Intelligence Brief
- Individual response drafts for all reviewed content
- 1× Manager Action Summary at end

**Client Obligation:**
- Provide review access (Google/TripAdvisor/Booking.com)
- 1 feedback call per week
- Written testimonial at completion

**Subtext CX Obligation:**
- Human-approved response drafts within 48h of batch processing
- Anonymized data rights for product improvement

**Exit Clause:**
Either party may cancel after Week 2 with 7 days notice

### Pilot Qualification Criteria

**Must have:**
- US-based English-language property
- ≥50 online reviews/month
- GM/Owner is primary contact
- No active contract with competing intelligence platform

**Disqualified:**
- Enterprise chains
- Properties with <30 reviews/month
- International properties (Phase 1)
- Active competitor contract

## 8. Demo Strategy

### Demo Format

**Live pipeline processing** with real review sample.

**NOT:** PowerPoint deck.  
**NOT:** Pre-recorded video.

**Show the actual pipeline running** on prospect's own property data (if possible).

### Demo Script Structure

**Part 1: Problem Framing (5 minutes)**
- "You're losing repeat customers for reasons your team never sees"
- Show 3 real reviews from prospect's property (pulled in advance with permission)
- Ask: "What operational failure do you see in these?"

**Part 2: Pipeline Processing (10 minutes)**
- Run ALA → EIP → ESS → HSI on those 3 reviews live
- Pause at ESS: show expression mode detection
- Pause at HSI: show masked emotion hypothesis
- Pause at BRA: show tier assignment + T3 dignity flag

**Part 3: Output Review (10 minutes)**
- Show RDA response draft + internal operational note
- Show Weekly Brief format (simulated with their data)
- Show Manager Action Summary format

**Part 4: Governance Emphasis (5 minutes)**
- Human approval gate (nothing publishes without review)
- Dignity governance (T3 escalation protocol)
- Brand voice control (Client Config table)

**Total:** 30 minutes (strict limit)

### Demo Success Criteria

**Prospect says one of:**
- "I've never seen that before"
- "This is the operational insight I need"
- "How do I get started?"

**Failure signals:**
- "Interesting, let me think about it" (polite no)
- "What's the ROI calculation?" (cost-focused, not value-focused)
- "Can you send me more information?" (not ready to commit)

### Demo Follow-Up

**Within 24 hours:**
- Send value prop one-pager
- Send pilot contract template
- Propose 2 calendar slots for kickoff call

**If no response in 3 days:**
- One follow-up email (non-pushy)
- No second follow-up (move to next prospect)

## 9. Pricing Communication Strategy

### Tier Communication (Landing Page + Deck)

**Show tier structure, NOT exact prices:**

**Tier 1 — Starter**
- Up to 200 reviews/month
- Weekly Brief + Individual responses
- "Starting at $1,500/month"

**Tier 2 — Professional**
- Up to 500 reviews/month
- + Manager Action Summary
- "Mid-tier pricing"

**Tier 3 — Enterprise**
- Unlimited reviews
- + Custom reporting + Priority SLA
- "Custom pricing"

### Exact Pricing Reveal

**Only after demo + qualification.**

Never on landing page. Never in first email.

**Rationale:** Price without context = commodity comparison. Demo first, price second.

### Pilot Pricing Exception

**Pilot 1:** $500 flat fee (or in-kind)

Communicated as: "We're looking for 1 design partner to help us refine the product. 30-day pilot at $500, then full pricing if you convert."

**Frame as exclusivity, not discount.**
