# SubtextCX Business Model

**How SubtextCX creates, delivers, and captures value**

---

## Value Proposition

### Core Promise

**"SubtextCX identifies the operational failures hidden in guest reviews and tells hospitality managers exactly what to fix — before those failures become revenue loss."**

### Category Definition

**Operational CX Intelligence** (distinct from survey tools, social listening, or basic review management)

### Primary Differentiator

**Masked and implicit emotion detection (ESS + HSI)** with governed AI response drafting — the market's commercially unclaimed frontier as of 2026.

### Secondary Differentiator

**Dignity-layer governance (T3 BRA)** for discrimination, cultural insensitivity, and high-stakes reputation events — no competitor has named or marketed this capability.

### Value Statement (LOCKED)

> "We identify the operational failures behind your worst reviews — and tell your managers exactly what to fix."

**Alternative positioning (luxury/boutique):**  
> "Protect your reputation by reading what your guests actually meant — not just what they wrote."

**Profile:**

## Value Delivery

### Three Client Deliverables

**Deliverable 1: Individual Response Drafts + Operational Notes**
- Cadence: Per review batch (2–3× per week)
- Agent: RDA
- Format: Public response + internal operational note
- Delivery: Email to GM/Owner

**Deliverable 2: Weekly Operational Intelligence Brief**
- Cadence: Every Monday morning
- Agent: SIA + MRA aggregation
- Content: Top emotional failure signals, operational risk areas, 3 recommended manager actions, trend vs. prior week
- Format: 1-page PDF or formatted email
- **Status:** FORMAT NOT YET DESIGNED (blocker before pilot)

**Deliverable 3: Monthly Manager Action Summary**
- Cadence: 1st of each month
- Agent: BRA action aggregation
- Content: Operational patterns, suggested protocol changes, staff touchpoints, correlation with rating movement
- Format: 2-page report
- **Status:** FORMAT NOT YET DESIGNED (complete before Month 2 of pilot)
