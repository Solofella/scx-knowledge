## Cost Control Principles

### Principle 1: Fixed Before Variable

**Prioritize fixed costs over variable costs.**

Infrastructure ($63/mo) and legal compliance ($185/yr) are non-negotiable. Marketing tools ($1,548/yr) are discretionary.

**Example:** If cash is tight, defer LinkedIn Premium ($960/yr) before cutting DigitalOcean.

---

### Principle 2: Validate Before Scale

**Marketing tools (LinkedIn Premium, email outreach) activate ONLY after Pilot 1 converts.**

Do not spend on outreach tools until warm network yields first client. Validate founder-led direct approach first.

---

### Principle 3: Free Tier Default

**Use free tiers until product-market fit is proven.**

- CRM: HubSpot free tier (not Starter $50/mo)
- Accounting: Wave free (not QuickBooks $108/yr)
- Review ingestion: Manual CSV upload (not PhantomBuster $228/yr)

Upgrade only when free tier is a genuine blocker.

---

### Principle 4: No Recurring Costs Without ROI Case

**Every monthly subscription requires justification.**

Before activating:
- What problem does this solve?
- What's the alternative (free or manual)?
- What's the ROI (time saved × hourly rate)?

**Example:** LinkedIn Premium ($80/mo) requires 2+ hours/week time savings on prospect research to justify.

## Expense Approval Workflow

### Expense Tier 1: Auto-Approved (<$50/month)

**Examples:**
- Canva Pro ($10/mo)
- Domain renewal ($25/yr)
- Small tool subscriptions

**No approval required** — just track and log.

---

### Expense Tier 2: Justification Required ($50–$200/month)

**Examples:**
- LinkedIn Premium ($80/mo)
- Email outreach tool ($39/mo)

**Required:**
- ROI case (time saved or revenue impact)
- Alternative considered and rejected
- 30-day test period before annual commit

---

### Expense Tier 3: Formal Decision Required (>$200/month or >$500 one-time)

**Examples:**
- LangSmith Plus ($39/mo ongoing)
- Pilot contract legal review ($300 one-time)
- Brand identity design ($250 one-time)

**Required:**
- Written justification
- Budget impact analysis
- Deferred vs. immediate decision
- Sign-off from Miguel (founder approval)
