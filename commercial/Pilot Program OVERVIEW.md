What VRYOH DOES with reviews

What it Detects (Signals)

Signal Direction - 

Positive / Negative / Ambiguous. The overall emotional direction — is this guest satisfied or not?

Expression Mode - 

Explicit / Implicit / Masked / Performative / Conflicted / Absent. How the emotion is being communicated — stated plainly, hidden between the lines, sarcastic, contradictory, or missing entirely.

Emotion Clarity - 

Clear / Diffuse / Fragmented / Ambiguous. Is the emotion coherent and understandable, or is the guest confused or conflicted about what they experienced?

Narrative Alignment -

High / Moderate / Low. Does what the guest said match what they actually experienced? High alignment means honest. Low alignment is a masked signal — a red flag.
